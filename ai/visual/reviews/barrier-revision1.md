# Eldritch Barrier — first revision critique

Opened the full 8.4s frame and the twelve ordered 0.1s crops covering 8.1–9.2s. This is sampled temporal inspection, not continuous video/audio playback.

Observed improvements: the illustrated face and cloak are visible through the cast; the ward encloses the feet and remains during the actual blocked strike and the post-hit state. No baked-in collision star appears at 8.4s. Same nine actions, same setup, same final state.

Significant remaining artistic defect: the uniformly thin, uninterrupted cyan arch resembles a diagram of a bell jar, especially at the 8.4s full battlefield view. The surface is too weak relative to its perimeter, and the response at absorption is barely distinguishable through the inherited cyan smoke. This is not accepted as the final visual treatment.

Correction: widen the shelter into a rounder enclosing volume; replace the uniform rim with a tapered, varied-value brush strip; use unequal transparent side facets referencing the card's cyan material; break the front ground lip so it does not double the occupancy ring; make the actual block-consumption response a visible pressure crease climbing the flanks. Preserve the clear center and all original combat feedback. One final capture follows this bounded refinement.
