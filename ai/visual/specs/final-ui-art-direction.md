# The Rift: final UI art direction — 2026-10-20

Continue the existing cohesion work; preserve the illustrated background, title
experiment, shared CardVisuals, TileMapLayer, PhantomCamera and actual combat FX.
No gameplay/balance changes or separate UI system.

Native baseline: `.qa-tmp/art-direction/before`, 1920×1080 window/viewport.
Inspected pause, menu and combat before editing; prior cohort captures informed
shop/reward/route direction. Final captures use the same seeded production scenes.

- Pause: calm interruption. Remove the colored wash and particles; keep scene
  context under a dark directional shade, serif title, clear Resume and secondary
  return action. Native focus must enter Resume and return to the previous control.
- Menu: an invitation. Left-aligned title/navigation, flatter ivory/brass type,
  stronger primary action, separate workshop footer, quiet build text. Preserve
  the original gateway illustration and hidden Echoes. Prioritize Continue when
  a saved expedition exists.
- Route: choose a crossing. Reuse painted slate in opaque, shallow hex remnants;
  differentiate glyphs, current gold/diamond and reachable teal. Thin future paths,
  emphasize accessible continuation. Place the current choices low to expose the
  journey ahead. Reveal encounter labels on focus; keep the legend optional.
- Shop: merchandise first. Enlarge existing painted cards through a measured
  shared presentation frame; remove redundant shelf panels; make purchase controls
  subordinate to art, identity and price. Keep native disabled/inspection behavior.
- Rewards: quick collection versus discovery. Compact currency rows, a larger
  discovery surface and larger existing card choices; no acquisition delay.
- Combat: concise information. Numeric plans remain conspicuous, movement/wait
  use their glyphs and full descriptions in the rail/tooltips. Reclaim unused
  inspection space and make camera instructions available on demand.

Acceptance: inspect complete compositions and disabled/inspection states, check
actual scaled bounds and rules fit, keyboard route activation and pause focus,
single-charge transactions and unchanged seeded combat outcomes. Use the existing
visual capture pipeline for representative active combat; sparse frames cannot
establish continuous animation or audio quality. No new bitmap assets or effects.
